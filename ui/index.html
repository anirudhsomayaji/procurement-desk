Legal Compliance Policy: Acme Corp's legal department prohibits four high-risk commercial terms in vendor proposals. Any appearance in the supplier's latest message immediately halts automated negotiation and escalates to Legal.

The 4 Prohibited Commercial Clauses

1. Automatic Renewal

Clauses stating the agreement continues year-to-year or auto-renews unless cancelled.

✗ "Contract auto-renews at the end of each annual period."
✓ Allowed: "Renewal strictly by mutual written agreement."
2. Upfront Payment > 30%

Advance deposits or upfront fees exceeding 30% of total contract value.

✗ "We require 40% upfront advance upon contract signing."
✓ Allowed: "25% advance with balance upon delivery."
3. Unlimited Liability for Acme

Clauses transferring uncapped, unlimited indemnification or consequential damages to Acme.

✗ "Acme agrees to accept unlimited liability for all damages."
✓ Allowed: "Standard liability capped at order value."
4. Supplier Exclusivity

Demands that Acme must purchase exclusively or appoint vendor as sole-source supplier.

✗ "Acme must agree to buy office supplies only from us."
✓ Allowed: "We are not asking for exclusivity."

Offer Normalization & Contract Overrides

Units, GST de-compounding, and supplier-specific terms
• Real Example: Catering Offer with 5% GST
Raw Supplier Quote: ₹315 per meal (inclusive of GST)
GST De-compounding: $p = \frac{₹315}{1 + 0.05} = \frac{₹315}{1.05}$
Normalized Price (p): ₹300 per meal (INR excl. GST)
At Round 2 ($L=₹310$), $p=₹300 \le L \implies$ Acceptable on Price! Proceed to Approval Limit Check.
• Contract Override: Arvind Office Supplies (GBP Rate)

Standard units-and-currency.md converts GBP at ₹106.0 per GBP. However, Arvind Office Supplies has a signed supplier contract specifying:

Supplier Contract Exchange Rate: ₹104.5 per GBP

The contract rate overrides the baseline table rate for this supplier, avoiding unfair currency penalties.

• Probation Supplier Policy (No Concessions)

For any supplier whose status is PROBATION, Acme Corp makes zero concessions:

$L(r) = \text{Target Price} \quad \forall r \in [1, R]$
